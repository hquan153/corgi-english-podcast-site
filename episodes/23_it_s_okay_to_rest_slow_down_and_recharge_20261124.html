Rest Without Guilt: Slow Down and Recharge | Corgi English Podcast

Do you feel busy all day and tired at night? Learn how to slow down and recharge with simple English words and calm daily habits.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can talk about resting in simple English and use small daily habits to recharge your body and mind.

A common problem and a tip

You practice English late at night even when your eyes feel heavy. You keep reading, but the new words do not stay in your head. Close your book after twenty minutes and sit quietly for five minutes. Drink some water and look out the window before you sleep.

Key words

  • recharge: to get your energy back again after working hard or feeling very tired
  • worn out: feeling very tired with almost no energy left after a long day
  • guilty: feeling bad because you think you did something wrong or not enough
  • balance: having a healthy mix of work and rest in your daily life
  • take a break: to stop working or studying for a short time to rest
  • slow down: to move and do things less fast so you feel more calm
