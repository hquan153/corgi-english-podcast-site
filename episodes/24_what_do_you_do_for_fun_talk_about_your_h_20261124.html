How to Share Your Hobbies in English | Corgi English Podcast

Do you freeze when someone asks what you do for fun? Learn easy ways to talk about hobbies in English with simple words and everyday stories.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can ask about free time and share your hobbies with simple, natural details.

A common problem and a tip

When someone asks what you do for fun, your mind goes blank. You feel shy and just say sleeping. Tonight, write three simple activities in your phone notes and read them for two minutes.

Key words

  • spare time: the free hours when you do not have to work or study
  • be into: to really like an activity and want to do it often
  • get into: to start liking a new activity and begin doing it
  • relaxing: making your body and mind feel calm and free from stress
  • join a club: to become a member of a group with a shared interest
  • unwind: to relax and let go of stress after work or study

Useful expressions

  • “lose track of time”: to enjoy something so much that you forget to check the clock

There is no complicated grammar there at all. Three short parts give your listener everything they need to enjoy your story. Once you practice this pattern twice, it feels automatic.
