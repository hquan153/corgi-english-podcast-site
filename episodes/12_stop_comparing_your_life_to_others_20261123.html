Stop Comparing Your Life: Feel Good Enough | Corgi English Podcast

Do you often look at other people and feel behind in life or English? In this calm episode, you will learn simple ways to stop comparing your life and feel proud of your own progress.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can talk about comparison in English, use six useful words, and try simple daily habits to focus on your own path.

From the creator

When I learn English or do anything, I often compare myself to others. If someone is behind me, I feel nothing. When someone is ahead of me, I feel weak and lose my motivation to keep going. Comparing yourself to others is not always bad if it gives you energy and focus. However, if it makes you feel sad and tired, it is not good for you.

Key words

  • compare: to look at two things or people to see how they are different
  • jealous: feeling sad or angry because someone else has something you want
  • progress: moving forward slowly and getting better at something over time
  • enough: having as much as you need, without needing to be perfect
  • own path: your personal way of living, learning, and growing at your speed
  • social media: websites and apps where people share photos, videos, and daily news

Useful expressions

  • “run your own race”: focus on your own goals and speed instead of watching other people
  • “the grass is always greener”: other people's lives often look better from the outside than they really are
  • “one step at a time”: do something slowly and calmly without rushing to the end

Only the Best Moments

Your Own English Speed
