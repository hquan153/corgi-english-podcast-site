Talk About Your Day in English Easily | Corgi English Podcast

Do you freeze when someone asks about your day? Learn simple ways to talk about your day in English with natural words and real scenes.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can share a short, natural story about your day using simple feelings and everyday phrases.

A common problem and a tip

When a friend asks how your day was, you might only say fine. Your mind goes blank, and the talk stops fast. Each night, spend two minutes saying three short sentences out loud about your day.

Key words

  • highlight: the best or most special moment that happened during your whole day
  • tiring: making you feel like you have very little energy left at the end
  • routine: the normal things you do in the same order almost every single day
  • so-so: not very good and not very bad, just normal or in the middle
  • busy: having a lot of things to do with very little free time
  • in the end: finally, after everything else happened, showing the last result of your day

Useful expressions

  • “Guess what”: a friendly way to share exciting or surprising news from your day
  • “It was a long day”: a natural way to say your day felt tiring or took a lot of energy
  • “By the way”: a phrase you use when you want to add one more small topic
  • “How was your day”: a warm question to ask someone about what happened to them today
