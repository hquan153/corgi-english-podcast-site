Why Your Evening Routine Shapes Your Tomorrow | Corgi English Podcast

Do you wake up tired after scrolling on your phone all night? A simple evening routine can help you rest well and start tomorrow with a calm mind.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can talk about your evening routine in English and use six everyday words and four expressions to plan a calmer night.

From the creator

My problem is that I often spend the whole evening scrolling on my phone and social media. I really want to do something useful, but I feel lazy, so I do not do it. My tip is that tonight affects tomorrow in a big way. Because I realize this, I try to use at least ten minutes each evening. In those minutes, I study, work, or do part of one simple task.

Key words

  • switch off: to turn off a screen or stop thinking about work for the night
  • wind down: to slowly relax your body and mind before you go to sleep
  • prepare: to get your clothes, bag, or things ready before you need them
  • fall asleep: to go from being awake to sleeping in your bed
  • wake up: to stop sleeping in the morning and open your eyes
  • rested: feeling fresh and full of energy after a good night of sleep

Useful expressions

  • “call it a day”: to stop working because you have done enough for today
  • “hit the hay”: to go to bed because you are ready to sleep
  • “sleep on it”: to wait until tomorrow before you make a big decision
  • “bright and early”: very early in the morning when you feel awake

Sayings for Restful Nights

Hit the hay means to go to bed because you are ready to sleep. For example, "Tom feels very tired, so he is going to hit the hay early." Next, sleep on it. Bright and early means very early in the morning when you feel awake. For example, "Lucy wakes up bright and early at six every morning."
