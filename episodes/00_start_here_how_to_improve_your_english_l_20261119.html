Key words

  • pause: To stop the audio for a short time so you can think or rest.
  • repeat: To listen to a word again or to say the same word after hearing it.
  • subtitles: The written words on your screen that show what the speakers are saying.
  • shadowing: A practice method where you speak right after the voice you hear.
  • catch a word: To hear and clearly understand a word when someone is speaking.
  • replay: To play an audio track or video again from an earlier point.
