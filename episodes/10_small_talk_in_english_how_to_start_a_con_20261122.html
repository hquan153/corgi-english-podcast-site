Small Talk in English: Start Any Chat | Corgi English Podcast

Do you freeze when you try to make small talk in English? Learn simple phrases and safe topics to start friendly chats at work or in your neighborhood.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can start a short, friendly conversation in English using safe topics, follow-up questions, and polite endings.

From the creator

After practicing alone in front of a mirror, speaking with other people made me feel very scared and shy. When I talk alone, I have time to think, fix mistakes, and not worry about understanding. When you dare to speak with others, you are already halfway to success. Do not worry too much, because the other person may also be a learner or understand how you feel. Starting one conversation is the true foundation for your English in the future.

Key words

  • small talk: a short, friendly chat about simple topics with someone you do not know well
  • awkward silence: a quiet moment in a chat when nobody knows what to say next
  • follow-up question: a second question you ask to show interest and keep a conversation going
  • safe topic: an easy subject like the weather or food that feels comfortable for everyone
  • body language: the way you smile, nod, or stand to show people how you feel
  • polite ending: a kind phrase you say when you need to leave a friendly conversation

Useful expressions

  • “break the ice”: to say or do something friendly so people feel relaxed at the start
  • “keep the ball rolling”: to help a conversation continue smoothly after it starts
  • “How is it going?”: a friendly, casual way to ask someone how they are doing
  • “Nice talking to you”: a warm, polite phrase to say when a short chat ends
