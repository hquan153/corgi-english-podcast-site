Ready to Step Out of Your Comfort Zone? | Corgi English Podcast

Do safe habits keep you from trying new things? Learn how to step out of your comfort zone with small, friendly steps and useful English phrases.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can talk about new challenges in English and use simple steps to leave your comfort zone safely.

A common problem and a tip

You know many English words, yet you stay quiet at a shop because you feel nervous about making a mistake. Tomorrow morning, look at a menu or a list and practice one short sentence out loud for two minutes. When you hear your own voice first, speaking to another person feels much easier.

Key words

  • comfort zone: a safe, easy place or habit where nothing feels hard or scary to you
  • challenge: something new or hard that tests your skills and helps you grow
  • nervous: feeling worried or a little afraid before you do something new
  • uncomfortable: not feeling relaxed or easy because a situation is strange or new
  • stretch: to push yourself a little past what feels easy and safe
  • worth it: good enough to pay for the time, work, or worry it took

Useful expressions
