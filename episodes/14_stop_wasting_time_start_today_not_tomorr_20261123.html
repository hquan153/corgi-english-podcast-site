How to Stop Wasting Time Today | Corgi English Podcast

Do you often wait until tomorrow and feel bad later? In this episode, learn simple ways to stop wasting time and take action today.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can explain why people put off tasks and use useful words and steps to take action today.

From the creator

I always have many plans, but with lots of free time, I think I will do them tomorrow. The next day, I think the same thing, so I wait one month, two months, or even one year. When we have a job to do, we do not need to finish it all at once. We can break it into smaller tasks and do a few each day. Maybe we do not start the tasks today, but we can plan those small tasks today.

Key words

  • put off: to wait to do something until a later time
  • deadline: the last day or time to finish a job
  • waste: to use your time or money in a bad way
  • to-do list: a short paper or note of tasks to finish
  • focus: to give all your attention to just one thing
  • progress: moving forward bit by bit toward a clear goal

Useful expressions

  • “better late than never”: it is good to start or finish something even if you waited
  • “no time like the present”: right now is the best time to begin a task
  • “one step at a time”: do one small part first instead of worrying about everything
  • “get the ball rolling”: to make a small start so the work begins to move

Common mistakes

  • Many people wait until they feel happy and full of energy before they start working. → Start the work first, because good feelings often show up after you begin.
  • Some people fill their day with tiny easy jobs and put off the important work. → Pick one important task in the morning and finish part of it before checking small things.
  • Many learners say "I wasted two hours to watch videos on my phone." → Say "I wasted two hours watching videos on my phone."

Using To After Waste
