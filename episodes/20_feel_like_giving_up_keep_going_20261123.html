When You Feel Like Giving Up, Keep Going | Corgi English Podcast

Do you feel like giving up when progress feels slow? In this calm episode, Emma and Ryan share simple ways to rest, see your real growth, and keep going.

Today's goal

After this episode, you can talk about hard days using six useful words and four encouraging expressions, and use small daily habits to keep going.

A common problem and a tip

Sometimes you practice English all week, but you still forget easy words when you speak. That heavy feeling makes you want to close your book and stop for good. When that happens, close your book for tonight and rest. Tomorrow morning, listen to one short English audio for just five minutes while you drink water.

Key words

  • give up: To stop trying to do something because it feels too hard for you.
  • keep going: To continue doing something even when you feel tired or have problems.
  • plateau: A flat time when you work hard, but your skill seems stuck.
  • progress: Small steps forward that show you are getting better over time.
  • stuck: A feeling when you try hard, but nothing seems to move forward.
  • burnout: A deep tired feeling when you push too hard without enough rest.

Useful expressions

  • “hang in there”: Do not stop yet, even though things feel hard right now.
  • “one step at a time”: Focus only on the next small action instead of the whole big goal.
  • “Rome was not built in a day”: Big skills take a long time to grow, so be patient with yourself.
  • “slow and steady”: Moving forward calmly every week works better than rushing too fast.
