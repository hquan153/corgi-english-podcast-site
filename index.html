Corgi English Podcast

Corgi English Podcast is a YouTube channel with calm, clear English listening practice for A2–B1 learners. Each episode is a slow, friendly conversation with on-screen subtitles.

Watch the channel on YouTube

About the app

The “Corgi English Podcast” app that asks for Google sign-in is a private upload tool. The channel owner uses it to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and subtitles to the channel's own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public.
